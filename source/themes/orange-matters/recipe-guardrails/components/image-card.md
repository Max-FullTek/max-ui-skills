## Orange Matters guardrails

- Default to `contain` so inspection output is not cropped. Reserve `cover` for explicit thumbnail use.
- Keep metadata short and use at most one badge by default; the image remains the visual focus.
- Prefer `infoLayout="below"`. Overlay bars must touch the lower card edges, remain fixed-height, and become more opaque only on hover or focus within the bar.
- Use compact actions, tokenized warm/charcoal surfaces, orange focus, and local overflow. Do not add generic white SaaS treatment or decorative copy.
